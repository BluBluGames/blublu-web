# Positioning

Status: Draft v1

## Working Notes
- The studio should be positioned as a commissioned 3D asset partner for indie games, not as a generic marketplace seller.
- The story should acknowledge the transition from 2D work to 3D production.
- Published packs on marketplaces are proof of capability, but the website should lead with custom commission work.

## Draft Positioning
- `blublu games` helps indie game creators get cohesive, stylized 3D assets made on commission, so they can ship demos, prototypes, and funded game projects faster without building an in-house art pipeline.

## USP Hypotheses
- Indie-friendly commissioning, not enterprise-style production.
- Style-consistent packs instead of one-off assets only.
- Marketplace-proven asset quality, now offered directly to clients.
- Flexible scope from small starter batches to large environment packs.
- Practical pricing bands that match indie budgets.
