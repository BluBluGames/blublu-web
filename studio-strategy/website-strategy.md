# Website Strategy

Status: Draft v5

## Working Notes
- Main conversion goal: commission inquiries.
- Secondary goals: showcase portfolio and link out to existing stores.
- The site should not feel like a generic asset marketplace landing page.
- Add explicit ICP validation step before finalizing homepage copy.
- Audience should be filtered by budget and seriousness, not just by studio size.

## Draft Strategy
- Lead with the commission offer, not the store links.
- Use portfolio and published packs as trust signals, not as the main CTA destination.
- Keep the page focused on one primary action: contact / brief request / quote request.
- Make the offer easy to compare by scope and budget.
- Use simple choices to reduce friction and decision overload.
- Include explicit keywords in the hero and service blocks so the site is understandable to both humans and AI search tools.
- Keep the commission path visible even if there is a secondary store/portfolio path.
- Treat Fiverr and GameDevFreelance as supporting acquisition channels, not as the core website story.
- Homepage should cover scope and exclusions at a high level, while deeper delivery/payment details can live in FAQ or a separate process section.
- The homepage should not try to explain every operational detail at once.

## Post-Inquiry Flow
- Do not send serious commission leads away to another marketplace by default.
- Keep the buyer in a direct email-led process so trust, scope, and momentum stay with the studio.
- Use platform links only when the client asks for platform protection, wants a familiar checkout flow, or comes in through that platform already.
- After the quote, the next step should be a short written scope and deposit, not a long sales funnel.

## Minimum Professional Signal
- The site should make it obvious that the studio understands scope, delivery, formats, and client workflow.
- The offer should read like a small production partner, not a hobbyist artist taking requests.
- The page should answer: what you make, for whom, in what style, for which engines, how the process works, and how to start.

## SEO Language
- custom 3D asset commissions
- stylized low poly 3D assets
- game-ready environment packs
- indie game outsourcing
- Unity and Unreal 3D assets

## Hero Offer
- Primary hero offer: custom 3D environment commissions for indie games.
- Supporting promise: stylized, low-poly, game-ready asset packs for Unity and Unreal.
- Hero tier: mid-size environment pack from $1500.
- Starter environment tier: from $600.
- Entry tier: smaller commission pack from $100+.
- Premium option: larger environment productions for funded teams and serious projects.

## Validation Step
- Confirm whether the primary buyer is a solo dev, tiny studio, or funded indie team.
- Confirm whether the main pain point is speed, style consistency, or production capacity.
- Confirm whether the most valuable offer is small commissions, medium packs, or larger environment packages.
- Confirm which promise should lead the homepage: budget fit, game-ready quality, or cohesive style.
- Confirm which buyer types are too low-intent to prioritize in messaging.
