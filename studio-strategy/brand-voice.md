# Brand Voice

Status: Draft v1

## Working Notes
- Brand voice should be optimized for clear search discovery and buyer understanding.
- The tone should be direct, specific, and easy for both humans and AI systems to classify.
- Avoid vague marketing language that hides what the studio actually makes.

## Draft Voice
- Clear
- Specific
- Game-dev focused
- Practical
- Professional but not corporate
- Friendly enough to feel approachable, but not casual in a way that weakens trust

## Voice Rules
- Say what you make in plain language.
- Use keywords buyers actually search for.
- Prefer concrete nouns over abstract hype.
- Avoid overexplaining or sounding like a hobbyist.

## Trust Signals
- Main trust signal: portfolio screenshots and render previews.
- Secondary trust signal: marketplace/store links placed later on the page.
- Process can be mentioned, but visual proof should do most of the work.
