# Services

Status: Draft v2

## Working Notes
- Primary offer is commission work.
- Marketplace sales remain a secondary channel and proof of work.
- Fiverr can be used later for per-asset offers.

## Draft Service Offer
- Small commission pack: around 5 assets from $100+, delivered in up to 3 business days.
- Starter environment pack: around 24-35 assets from $600, delivered in up to 10 business days.
- Mid-size environment pack: around 24-35 assets from $1500, with more scope, more variation, or a larger environment set.
- Large commission pack: around 140-200 assets from about $3200-$3500, with a longer production window around 2 months or more.
- Custom style or environment pack tailored to the client's game direction.
- Potential future add-on: per-asset Fiverr-style offer for clients who want smaller, more modular work.

## Draft Service Framing
- Start with a clear scope.
- Show a price range tied to asset count and project size.
- Make the commission path easier than browsing a marketplace and assembling assets piecemeal.
- Use the mid-size environment pack as the hero offer.
- Keep the $100 / 5-asset offer as an entry point, not the hero offer.
- Treat the large pack as a premium production engagement for serious teams.

## What Makes the Offer Look Professional
- A clear asset scope with examples of what is included.
- A clear engine target such as Unity, Unreal, or another supported runtime.
- A clear timeline or delivery window.
- A clear revision policy.
- A clear handoff format such as `.fbx`, `.blend`, `.png`, textures, and any engine-specific exports.
- A clear commercial usage statement.
- A clear payment structure such as deposit, milestone, or full upfront for smaller jobs.
- A clear communication process for brief, feedback, and approval.
- A clear list of exclusions, especially characters, rigging, animation, and anything outside the offer.

## Channel Notes
- Direct commissions should be the primary sales path.
- Fiverr and GameDevFreelance can be used as secondary lead sources or lower-friction entry channels, but they should not define the brand or homepage positioning.
- Marketplace stores remain proof of work and trust signals, not the main sales mechanism for commission clients.

## Preferred Sales Flow
- Client discovers the studio and sends an inquiry.
- Studio replies with a short clarification message and a rough quote or range.
- If the fit is good, send a simple written scope, timeline, and payment terms by email.
- Collect a deposit before work starts.
- Keep the rest of the project inside email or a shared document unless the client explicitly prefers a marketplace platform.
- Use Fiverr or GameDevFreelance only as an alternate checkout / trust option if the buyer wants platform protection or already came from there.
