# Competitors

Status: Draft v1

## Working Notes
- External market scan suggests several adjacent competitor patterns:
  - solo stylized prop artists
  - low-poly asset storefronts
  - larger game art studios offering commission/outsource work
- Their websites usually emphasize engine-ready, optimized, cohesive assets, not just isolated models.

## Observed Competitor Patterns
- `jvdbstudio.com` presents a stylized/low-poly prop artist with game-ready assets and a portfolio-first website.
- `oricutstudios.itch.io` positions stylized low-poly assets for games, prototypes, game jams, and interactive projects.
- `mrmgame.com` combines marketplace presence with outsourcing, emphasizing stylized game assets and custom work.
- `standout7.itch.io` frames itself around production-ready modular low-poly kits for game developers.
- `ithappystudios.com` emphasizes high-quality low-poly models for small teams and indie developers, with broad engine support and strong usability.

## Competitor Implications
- The best-looking market segment is not generic 3D art buyers; it is indie creators who need cohesive, game-ready assets fast.
- Studio positioning should emphasize style consistency, practical integration, and commission flexibility.
- We should differentiate from pure asset stores by making the custom commission path the headline offer.
