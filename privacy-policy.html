What this site collects

If you contact BluBluGames by email, the information you send is used only to reply to your inquiry, prepare a quote, or discuss a commission.

External links

The site links to external services such as social media and marketplace profiles. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies and tracking

This website is intended to run without cookies or client-side analytics. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.
